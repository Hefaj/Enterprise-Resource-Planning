# Bulk name and price changes

One operation can set a name or price on many products selected from the list.

## Who can perform the operation

Permission for the relevant product change and product list access are required.

## Where to find the feature

Select at least one record on the product list and choose the name or price action from the action toolbar.

## How to perform the operation

Check the selection scope, enter the new value in the operation dialog, and confirm. Track progress in the tasks panel.

## Result

Every product is processed separately. The tasks panel shows successes and errors, and the list refreshes changed records.

## Limitations and special cases

Partial success is valid: an error on one product does not roll back changes saved for other products.
