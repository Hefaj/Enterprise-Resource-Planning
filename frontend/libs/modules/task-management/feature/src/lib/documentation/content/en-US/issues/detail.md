# Issue details

The issue page combines description, state, fields, comments, activity, files, links, tags, and work time.

## Who can perform the operation

Reading requires access to the issue's project. Editing, transitions, and content management require update permission.

## Where to find the feature

Open a key from a list or board, or use a direct address ending with a key such as `issue/DEV-123`.

## How to perform the operation

Edit individual values in their sections. Choose an allowed transition and complete any required-fields dialog. Add comments, attachments, and time entries in their sections.

## Result

Each saved change refreshes the page and is represented in history or the activity stream.

## Limitations and special cases

A missing transition means the workflow does not allow it. Links use the readable issue key rather than the technical UUID.
